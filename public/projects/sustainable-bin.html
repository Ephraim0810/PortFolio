Design Project 02 · National Design Project · 2023

A recycling bin built to make sustainability the path of least resistance.

My first national-level design competition. An extension of workshops run with the Bank of Singapore and Republic Polytechnic, we tackled the problem of motivating recycling — designing for the adults who do most of it, and the friction that quietly puts them off.

Year2023
CompetitionNational Design Project
PartnersBank of Singapore · Republic Polytechnic
RoleDesigner · Team of 4
AwardCertificate of Merit

Process

We anchored on Singapore's iconic Blue Bin and asked a simple question — what would make adults reach for it more often? Three months of research, three rounds of user testing, one final prototype.

01 · EMPATHY

The Blue Bin question

Studied existing recycling methods in Singapore, then ran interviews with adults to understand what stopped them from recycling consistently — and what would actually move the needle.

02 · IDEATE

Sketch, rate, repeat

Generated a wide spread of ideas and rated them across realism, cost, behavioural impact, and how naturally they fit into a household routine.

03 · PROTOTYPE

Three rounds of testing

Sketched, built, and iterated through three rounds with real users — each round forcing us to throw away an assumption we had walked in with.

From poster to physical prototype

Our first prototype was a poster reminding people why to recycle. We quickly learned that nobody needs reminding — they need the bin itself to be less of a hassle.

What made it into the bin

Each feature came out of a specific moment in user testing — not a brainstorming session.

01Partitions to organise different trash types
02Flexible, swappable compartments
03Auto cup-washer & dryer
04Built-in reminder poster surface
05Ventilation holes to control smell
06Wheels for easy movability
★

Certificate of Merit · National Design Project

Recognised at the national level — but the most useful win was understanding that good behavioural design is mostly about removing friction, not adding messaging.
