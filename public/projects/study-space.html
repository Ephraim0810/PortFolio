Design Project 01 · 2023

A study space rethought for the way students actually work.

My first official school-based design project in Secondary 3. After a few weeks of mentorship on design principles, our team set out to refurbish a part of the school — and after talking to peers, we kept hearing about the same neglected room: the study space.

Year2023
RoleDesigner · Team of 4
ProgrammeDesign Elective
ToolsAutoCAD · Foam-core · Pencil
Outcome3D visualisation & quotation

Process

We followed the design-thinking loop end-to-end — empathy, ideation, prototyping, and testing — and built a quotation for the whole space at the end.

01 · EMPATHY

Listening before designing

Interviewed peers about under-used spaces. The school's existing study spaces kept coming up as not elaborate enough — that became our brief.

02 · IDEATE

Sketching, rating, narrowing

Generated a wide range of ideas, rated them across feasibility, cost, and student impact, and converged on a small set worth testing.

03 · PROTOTYPE

From foam-core to AutoCAD

Built a physical prototype of a partitioning table, tested it with real users, then rebuilt the whole space as a 3D visualisation in AutoCAD.

From sketch to model

A few of the rough sketches and the prototype table that shaped the final concept.

The final space

Six features made it into the final concept — each tied directly back to a piece of feedback from the empathy stage.

01Bookshelves stocked with textbooks
02Partitioned individual study tables
03Beanbags & movable stools
04Whiteboards for group discussion
05Dedicated room for group study
06First-rate furnishing materials
◷

Benched, but useful

The proposal was eventually shelved due to school budget constraints — but the process taught me more about real-world design than the outcome ever could have.
